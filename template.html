How to read the indicators
  • RSI (14) — momentum oscillator 0–100. Below 30 the stock is oversold (potential rebound), above 70 overbought (potential pullback). The arrow shows the change over the last week.
  • P/E (PER) — price / trailing 12-month earnings. Lower = cheaper; compare within a sector (banks ~6–10, luxury ~20–30). Fwd P/E uses expected earnings: if it's lower than P/E, earnings are expected to grow.
  • PEG — P/E divided by earnings growth. Below 1 is often considered attractive.
  • SMA50 / SMA200 — 50 and 200-day moving averages. Price above SMA200 = long-term uptrend; SMA50 above SMA200 = "golden cross" regime.
  • MACD — trend-following momentum. Histogram turning positive is a bullish signal.
  • %B (Bollinger) — position inside the Bollinger bands: < 0 below lower band (stretched down), > 1 above upper band.
  • From 52w high — drawdown from the 1-year high. Deep drawdowns on quality stocks can be opportunities — or value traps.
  • Div. yield, ROE (return on equity, >15% is good), margin, debt/equity — quality and income metrics.
  • Upside — distance to the analysts' mean price target. Reco — consensus 1 (strong buy) to 5 (sell).
  • Score — weighted mix of 4 pillars: Value 35%, Momentum 25%, Quality 20%, Analysts 20%. ≥70 Strong buy, ≥60 Buy, ≥45 Hold, else Avoid. Click a row to see exactly which rules fired.
Data: Yahoo Finance (may be delayed or incomplete). This is a mechanical screen, not financial advice — always do your own research.
